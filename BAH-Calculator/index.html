About the BAH Calculator

PCSUnited’s BAH & Base Pay Calculator gives military users a quick estimate of monthly compensation by combining projected BAH, estimated Base Pay, and BAS.

Adjust pay grade, years in service, duty location, and dependency status to preview housing-related compensation before deeper affordability and housing analysis.

BAH Calculator FAQ

What does this BAH calculator estimate?

It estimates projected monthly BAH, Base Pay, BAS, and total monthly military compensation using the selected duty location, grade, dependency status, and years in service.

Does BAH change by location and dependents?

Yes. BAH can vary significantly by housing market and whether the member has dependents.

Why does the calculator also show Base Pay and BAS?

BAH is shown first as the primary housing allowance. Base Pay and BAS are displayed separately to provide additional military compensation context.

Planning estimate

2023–2025 basic pay is reconstructed from the official 2026 baseline using published annual general pay adjustments and is not an archived historical pay table. Future years are planning estimates: 3.6% for 2027, 3.3% for 2028, 3.2% for 2029, 3.0% for 2030, and the selected long-range assumption beginning in 2031. The selected retirement rank is applied across the full High-3 period. Results show gross retired pay before taxes, SBP, or other deductions. Actual retired pay may differ.

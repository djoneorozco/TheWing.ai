About the Retirement & VA Calculator

PCSUnited’s Retirement & VA Disability Calculator provides a planning estimate of projected monthly military retirement pay and VA disability compensation.

Adjust retirement system, retired rank, years of service, VA disability rating, and dependent profile to see how those inputs affect the projected monthly estimate.

Retirement & VA Calculator FAQ

What does this calculator estimate?

It estimates projected monthly military retirement pay, VA disability compensation, and the combined monthly income produced by those estimates.

Does retirement pay change by retirement system?

Yes. The calculator supports High-3 and the Blended Retirement System and also uses the selected retired rank and years of service.

What affects estimated VA disability compensation?

The VA portion uses the selected disability rating and dependent profile provided in the calculator.

Is this an official retirement or VA determination?

No. This calculator is intended for planning and estimation. Official retirement and VA benefit determinations come from the appropriate government agencies.

What do these numbers mean?
Calculator loads
A calculator connected to our tracker. Opening it counts even without an input change. Reloading counts again; this is not a unique-person count.
Visits with an input change
Loaded visits with at least one deliberate change. The percentage uses only visits captured by this updated tracker.
Input changes
Committed edits, with a continuous slider movement grouped into one change.
Active time
Estimated time after starting, while the calculator is visible and the page has focus. Timing pauses after 30 seconds without interaction. Updates are sent in small batches.
Updated result
The calculator reported a successful result after an input change. Some default values may still be present.
Dates
Visits are grouped by their load date in UTC. A visit continuing past midnight remains with its load date.

What do these numbers mean?
Usage session
A visit in which someone deliberately changes a calculator input. Opening the page alone does not count. Reloading starts a new session.
Input changes
Committed edits, with a continuous slider movement grouped into one change.
Active time
Estimated time after starting, while the calculator is visible and the page has focus. Timing pauses after 30 seconds without interaction. Updates are sent in small batches.
Updated result
A valid scenario appeared after an input change. Some default values may still be present.
Dates
Sessions are grouped by their start date in UTC. A session continuing past midnight remains with its start date.

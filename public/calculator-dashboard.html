What do these numbers mean?
Changed an input
A visitor deliberately changed a calculator value. Simply opening the page does not count.
Updated result
A valid scenario appeared after an input change. This does not confirm the person entered every detail or completed a personal estimate.
Active time
Estimated time after starting, while the calculator is visible and the page has focus. Timing pauses after 30 seconds without interaction. This is separate from GA4’s average engagement time.
Time per starter
Total active time divided by people with a start event in the selected period. Visits crossing the date boundary can affect this estimate.
People
GA4’s reported users for the selected period, not identified individuals. Consent, browser restrictions, and repeat devices can affect counts.
Google paid
Sessions attributed to google / cpc. This helps isolate ad traffic, but does not include every possible paid visit or prove the ads are profitable.

PT and standalone Disability Rating only. The combined VA/retirement page is outside this report. Exclude your own testing before evaluating ads. This report cannot recover activity that was never recorded.

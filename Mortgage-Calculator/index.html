Mortgage & Budget Questions

What is included in the monthly payment?

Principal and interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA and applicable mortgage insurance. Maintenance, utilities and closing costs are separate.

Is the monthly remainder a financial-health score?

No. It is your entered take-home income minus the proposed housing payment and your other entered expenses. It does not assess all your assets, liabilities or emergency reserves.

Are the interest rate and PMI lender quotes?

No. Enter a quoted interest rate and PMI amount when available. Blank rate or PMI fields use labeled planning assumptions from TheWing. FHA estimates are not supported in this release.

Does this replace lender approval?

No. Confirm loan terms, VA fee exemption and eligibility with your lender. Your budget inputs are optional and remain in memory on this page.

Sources & methodology

Established measures retain their original questions and scoring. Housing exercises, comparison rules, and knowledge checks are original RE-Psych content. The combined application has not been validated as a homebuyer prediction test.

How your information is handled

Answers stay in this browser session unless you choose “Save on this device” or download a report. There are no analytics, external fonts, AI calls, or data submissions. Saved drafts are stored unencrypted in your browser; use a private device. Opening an external source leaves this app.
